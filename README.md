# 💪 Forge — Fitness Tracker

> A simple fitness tracker to log workouts, track weekly consistency and unlock milestones.

**Live:** https://project-zero-lab.github.io/forge-fitness-tracker/

**Built by:** Suraj Kandpal · Project Zero × Claylab Education Foundation (Cohort 1)

## What it does
- Logs workouts with date, focus, exercise, sets, reps, weight and notes
- Shows workouts this week, total sessions and exercises logged
- Tracks daily water intake with a glass counter
- Charts the last 7 days of sessions
- Includes a searchable exercise library with one-tap add to log
- Unlocks milestones at 1, 5 and 10 sessions

## How to use
1. Open the live link
2. Tap **Log workout** and fill in the details
3. Tap **Save workout**
4. Check **History** to review or delete entries
5. Open **Exercises** and tap **Add to log** to pre-fill an exercise
6. Use **Progress** to view your weekly chart and milestones
7. On **Overview**, tap **Add glass** to track water
